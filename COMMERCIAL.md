# Commercial Licensing

Rusty is free software under the [GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or
later](./LICENSE) (AGPL-3.0-or-later). For most open-source and internal use, the
AGPL is all you need, and it costs nothing.

This page is for the case where the AGPL's terms **don't** fit.

## When you need a commercial license

The AGPL is strong copyleft with a network clause. If you **modify Rusty and run
it to provide a service over a network**, the AGPL requires you to offer the
complete corresponding source of your modified version to that service's users.
Redistributing Rusty (modified or not) as part of a larger work also carries the
AGPL's source-availability and same-license obligations.

A commercial license may be the right fit if you want to:

- **Embed Rusty in a closed-source product** you distribute, without releasing
  your own source under the AGPL.
- **Run a modified Rusty as a network service** without the AGPL's requirement to
  publish your modifications to users.
- **Ship Rusty inside a proprietary appliance or SDK** under your own terms.

If none of those describe you — for example you're using stock Rusty internally,
building open-source software, or contributing back — you almost certainly do
**not** need a commercial license. The AGPL already covers you.

## What a commercial license grants

A commercial license grants **relief from the AGPL's copyleft and
network-disclosure obligations** for the agreed scope of use. It does **not** by
itself include a warranty, support, indemnity, or a service-level agreement —
those can be negotiated as separate terms, but are not implied by the license.
Rusty's technical claims remain exactly as stated in its documentation:
*proven on the declared domain, sameness never safety.* A commercial license
changes your **licensing** obligations, not what the software guarantees.

Terms (per-deployment, per-seat, per-product, or site-wide) are negotiable to fit
the use.
